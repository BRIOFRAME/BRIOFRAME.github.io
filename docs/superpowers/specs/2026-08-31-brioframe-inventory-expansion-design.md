# BRIOFRAME Inventory Expansion Design — Superseded

This earlier inventory-count design is retained only as historical project context. Its former fixed-count target is retired and must not be used for implementation decisions.

The active direction is the 35-template premium catalog established through Phase 4. Future additions are approved individually or in small quality-led waves based on commercial usefulness, SEO opportunity, customer demand, and genuine design differentiation.

## Requirements That Still Apply
- Preserve verified Shopify destinations exactly.
- Never invent a Shopify purchase URL; unverified products remain `Preview`.
- Every released record needs a matching preview and responsive working demo.
- Variants must be materially different, not cosmetic reskins.
- Public demos remain sanitized and safe to expose.
- Keep BRIOFRAME extensible for future premium modules and integrations.

See `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-03-brioframe-quality-first-release-strategy.md` for the active catalog strategy.
