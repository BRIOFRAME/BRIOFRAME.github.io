About the collection

We would rather know seven houses well than seventy by name.

SOLARA began in 2011 with a single house on the Amalfi Coast and a simple idea: that a villa holiday should feel like staying with a friend who happens to have the most beautiful home you've ever seen — and a household to match.

Our philosophy

  1. I

    Fewer, better houses

    A villa joins the collection only after a full season of stays. Most never make it past the first visit.

  2. II

    People before property

    We choose houses for their households as much as their views. A great chef matters more than a bigger pool.

  3. III

    Quiet, not ostentatious

    Luxury is privacy, time and things done properly — not gold taps. We say no to anything that feels like a show.

Since 2011

Fifteen years, seven houses

We grow slowly, one house and one coast at a time, and only where we can be there in person.

  1. 2011

    Villa Aurelia, Amalfi Coast. The first house, and the household that set our standard.

  2. 2014

    The Cyclades. Casa Lefka on Santorini, followed by Villa Oriel on Mykonos.

  3. 2017

    Bali. Villa Serai on the Uluwatu cliffs, then Villa Lumen in the Sidemen valley.

  4. 2021

    The Maldives. Kanu Water Residence, our first home over water.

  5. 2024

    The Andaman Coast. Villa Halden, facing the karsts of Krabi.

The SOLARA standard

112 points, every season

Before each season, a member of our team stays in every villa and checks it against the same standard. These are some of the things we look for.

Arrival

  • Met at arrivals, never a sign in a crowd
  • Villa briefing in the car
  • Fridge stocked to your list

Household

  • Same core staff each season
  • Chef menu tasting by our team
  • Discretion training for every role

House

  • Linen, mattresses and towels to hotel spec
  • Reliable connectivity in every room
  • Blackout in all bedrooms

Safety

  • Pool and balcony checks for children
  • Medical contacts and nearest clinic
  • Fire and security systems tested
