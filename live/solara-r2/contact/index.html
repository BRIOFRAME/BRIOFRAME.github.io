    Questions

    Before you write

    Is there a live availability calendar?

    No. Every date is confirmed personally with the house, which lets us hold dates that aren't publicly released and avoid double bookings. Most enquiries receive an answer the same day.

    How do payments work?

    Once you confirm a proposal, we send a secure payment link from our payment provider. We never ask for card details by email or through this website.

    Are staff included in the rate?

    Each villa lists the household included in its rate. Additional services — such as extra chef meals or childcare — are quoted separately in your proposal.

    Can we bring children or pets?

    Children are welcome in every villa, and we'll prepare cots, high chairs and pool fencing where needed. Pets are considered case by case — ask your planner.

    How do I list my property with SOLARA?

    Choose "A property owner" above and tell us about your home. Our owners team replies within two working days.
