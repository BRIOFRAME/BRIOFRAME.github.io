One planner, from the first call to the last guest home

Your event planner coordinates the villa, suppliers, guest travel and the timetable of the day, and is on site throughout.

  1. 12 months

    Questions

    Before you enquire

    Can we have a legal ceremony at the villa?

    Rules differ by country. In some destinations a civil ceremony must take place at a town hall first; your planner will explain what applies and arrange the paperwork with local registrars.

    How many guests can a villa host?

    Each villa has its own limits for seated dinners and standing receptions, shown in the venue finder above. Some houses host in-house guests only, and every event needs the owner's approval.

    Where do guests stay if the party is larger than the villa?

    We reserve rooms at nearby hotels and arrange transfers so everyone arrives together for the ceremony and dinner.

    Is there a curfew for music?

    Most villas end amplified music at 23:00 under their house rules. Acoustic music and quiet dinners can continue later.

    What does it cost?

    Every event is priced individually: the villa stay, an event fee set by the owner, and suppliers at cost. Your proposal lists each item before anything is booked.
