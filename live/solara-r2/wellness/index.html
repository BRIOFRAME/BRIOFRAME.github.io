Wellness & spa

Wellness,
brought to you.

Therapists, teachers and trainers who come to the villa — so a treatment happens where you already feel at home.

See the treatment menu Book through the concierge

In the villa

Five ways to rest

Every villa can host treatments. Some have their own spa pavilions and resident therapists; the villa comparison below shows which.

01 · Massage

In-villa massage

Deep-tissue, relaxing or traditional techniques from each destination — Balinese in Bali, Thai on the Andaman coast — given in your suite, on a shaded terrace or by the pool.

02 · Movement

Yoga & movement

Sunrise yoga on the deck, Pilates and mobility, or a personal trainer who plans sessions around your usual routine. Mats and equipment come with the teacher.

03 · Rituals

Signature rituals

Longer treatments rooted in local practice: warm stones, a Balinese spice wrap and flower bath, or a hammam scrub at Casa Lefka.

04 · Set-up

A private treatment room, anywhere

Your therapist brings a heated table, linen, oils and candles, and sets up a quiet treatment space wherever you choose. Couples' treatments need only two tables side by side.

Request a set-up

05 · Recovery

Recovery

Post-flight lymphatic massage, assisted stretching and contrast water in the villa pool — useful after a long journey or a day on the trails.
