The house

Kanu stands at the end of its own jetty on the edge of a Baa Atoll lagoon, three pavilions linked by boardwalks over glass-clear water. The main pavilion has a curved wall of windows to the ocean and steps straight down into the lagoon.

A dedicated host, chef and boat crew look after the residence. Mornings are for the house reef and the sandbank; evenings for dinner on the deck, with the manta season bringing some of the atoll's best snorkelling from June.

  • Private jetty and lagoon steps
  • Glass-floor living pavilion
  • Dedicated host, chef and boat
  • Seaplane transfer included

At the villa

The spaces

Arrival & grounds

By seaplane, then jetty

The seaplane lands beside the atoll, and the residence's boat brings you to the end of Kanu's private jetty, where the host is waiting. Luggage goes ahead; you walk the boardwalk over the lagoon.

Living

Glass-floor living pavilion

A curved wall of windows to the ocean and a glass floor panel over the lagoon. Steps from the deck lead straight into the water.

Dining

Dining pavilion

A separate pavilion for dinner, with a table on the deck for evenings and a chef who plans menus around the day's catch.

Pool & terrace

Sunset deck and plunge pool

A wide deck with parasols and daybeds, an overwater plunge pool and lagoon steps for the house reef.

Spa & wellness

Overwater treatment deck

Treatments are given on a shaded deck over the lagoon by the resident therapist, morning or evening.
